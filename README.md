Original 0x88-board chess engine in one packed Python file
(`main.py`, 4,095 bytes — the 4,096-byte cap, 1 byte to spare).

## Design

- Negamax alpha-beta + quiescence (captures/promotions, all evasions
  when in check), transposition table, MVV-LVA ordering, iterative
  deepening to a **fixed node budget of 40,000 nodes/move** —
  deterministic (no clock, no unseeded randomness), so a replayed
  trial plays the same moves.
- Tapered PeSTO (mg/eg) piece-square tables (Ronald Friederich /
  RofChade, used as evaluation data, as the brief suggests). Endgame
  tables are kept only for pawn and king; N/B/R/Q reuse their
  middlegame tables in the endgame.
- Perft verified on startpos, Kiwipete, pos3, pos4, pos5 (depth 1–3,
  all exact); Zobrist keys verified consistent over 200 random move
  sequences; mate-in-1 and win-the-queen tactics pass.

## Files

- `main.py` — the packed entrypoint (makefun-compress/v1 LZMA stub).
- `src/engine.py` — readable source (not committed).
- `src/engine.min.py` — minified source, generated by
  `scripts/minify.py` from `src/engine.py`.
- `scripts/minify.py` — the deterministic minifier.

Rebuild: `python3 scripts/minify.py`, then (separate command)
`node <kit>/cli/makefun.mjs compress src/engine.min.py --out main.py`.

## Local measurement (practice trials, house anchors)

Anchors: depth-2 (855), depth-3 (1073), depth-4 (1199), nodes-100k
(1392), nodes-250k (1497), nodes-500k (1596). Floor to enter the
final: 1,154. House baseline (sunfish 4KB): ~1,365 mean.

| Trial | Engine | Result | Elo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| trial-0 | pre-cut (killers, full eg) | 3W 0D 3L | ~1,279 |
| trial-1 | pre-cut (killers, full eg) | 3W 3D 0L | ~1,540 |
| trial-2 | **final `main.py`** (packed) | 4W 1D 1L | ~1,545 |

Mean of all three trials: ~1,455. The final packed engine drops
killer ordering, cross-depth root ordering and the N/B/R/Q endgame
tables to fit 4 KiB, yet trial-2 (4.5/6, ~1,545) matches the pre-cut
engine's best trial — the cuts cost nothing measurable at the 40k
node budget. Trial-2 won 3 games by checkmate and 1 by material
adjudication (lead ≥5), drew 1 (insufficient material), lost 1.
